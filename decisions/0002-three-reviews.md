# 0002 Three reviews, one per part of the template

**Decision:** the evidence is split into three multivocal reviews, one per part of the topic: its types, its display
at depth, its storage.

Status: accepted, 2026-10-29. Shape: [decision record](../shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- The README asked four questions: genres, depth, self-reference, graphs.
- Two sibling repositories were writing reviews on the same day, and some works (advance organizers, Swales's moves,
  structured proofs) were already reviewed in the family.

## Decision

- information-typing answers genres; progressive-disclosure answers depth; document-model answers self-reference and
  graphs.
- Each work is reviewed in one repository only. The boundaries were agreed by message with the sessions writing the
  siblings, and every key was checked by script against their bibliographies.

## Consequences

- 218 sources, with no work reviewed twice across the family.
- Facts the three reviews share (method, counting databases, markings) live once, in the
  [evidence README](../evidence/README.md#method).
- The cost: a reader of one review follows links to the others and to the siblings for what is held there.
