# 0001: Call the unit a topic

**Decision:** the unit this repository defines is called a topic, the word of DITA and of topic-based authoring.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-29. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/d408349e4bc0f8e9ac7672645bd2aa393e5e6704/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- The repository was opened as recursive-atom, and its unit was called a node: both were our words, chosen before the
  review asked what the field calls the thing.
- The frames of the three reviews answered. Practice and DITA say **topic**, with derivation called specialization;
  markup research says content object (OHCO); studies of trees on screen say node.

## Decision

- The unit is a topic; the repository is reader-ergonomics-topic, in the family's naming scheme.
- The reviews take the field's names: information-typing, progressive-disclosure, document-model.
- "Node" stays where it is the field's own word: a node of a tree on screen, or of a markup model.

## Consequences

- A reader from technical writing recognises the unit; GitHub redirects the old repository address, but not the old
  site.
- "Topic" had also meant a review subject in the evidence files; those uses now say review, so the word keeps one
  meaning.
- Risk: a reader may assume DITA's rules, which are not all ours. The topic's own rules are in
  [shapes/topic.md](../shapes/topic.md).
